GammaCoin (GAMMA)
Overview
GammaCoin (GAMMA) is a deflationary token built on the Polygon network with advanced tokenomics, automatic liquidity generation, and anti-whale protection mechanisms. With a total supply of 47 million tokens, GammaCoin combines the best features of modern cryptocurrencies to create a sustainable and community-driven ecosystem.

Features
Transaction Fees (8% Total)
Every transaction is subject to the following fees:

3% Liquidity Fee: Automatically adds to liquidity pool, increasing price stability
2% Marketing Fee: Funds ongoing marketing efforts and project development
2% Reflection Fee: Redistributes to all holders through token burns
1% Burn Fee: Permanently removes tokens from circulation, creating deflationary pressure
Anti-Whale Protection
Maximum Transaction Limit: 2% of total supply per transaction
Maximum Wallet Limit: 3% of total supply per wallet
Excluded Addresses: Creator, contract, and liquidity pools are excluded from these limits
Automatic Liquidity Generation
Tokens collected from transaction fees are automatically converted to MATIC
Half of the converted MATIC is paired with tokens to add liquidity
Liquidity is locked in the pool, increasing price stability and reducing volatility
Security Features
Ownership Controls: Critical functions are protected by ownership verification
Anti-Bot Measures: Trading can be enabled at a specific time to prevent bots
Emergency Functions: Ability to recover stuck tokens or MATIC in case of emergencies

Smart Contract
The GammaCoin smart contract includes the following key functions:

enableTrading(): Enables trading after initial setup
addInitialLiquidity(uint256 tokenAmount): Adds initial liquidity to the DEX
transferFromContract(address to, uint256 amount): Transfers tokens from contract to specified address
updateFees(uint256 _liquidityFee, uint256 _marketingFee, uint256 _reflectionFee, uint256 _burnFee): Updates fee structure
excludeFromFees(address account, bool excluded): Excludes/includes address from fees
